Comparing your records

  1. Click the Group button on any row to mark it. First tap = Group A, second tap = Group B, third tap = ungrouped again.
  2. For example, to compare your first week against your second week, tag every row from days 1–7 as Group A and every row from days 8–14 as Group B.
  3. Then pick what you want to know below:
Sum — adds Group A and Group B together, for a combined total.
A − B — how much more (or less) Group A earned compared to Group B.
B − A — the same comparison, the other way round.
% Change — how much Group B is up or down compared to Group A, as a percentage. Group A is always treated as the starting point.
